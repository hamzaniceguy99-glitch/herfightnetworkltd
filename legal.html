What we provide

Her Fight Network provides the activities of a sports club and other sports activities provided by HER FIGHT NETWORK LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 15240754. IMPORTANT — HEAD INJURY AND CONCUSSION: the club applies the UK Concussion Guidelines for Grassroots Sport. Where a concussion is suspected, however mild, the participant is REMOVED FROM THE SESSION IMMEDIATELY AND DOES NOT RETURN TO ACTIVITY THAT DAY, on the principle that if there is any doubt the participant sits out. Immediate medical attention is sought where there are any red flags, including loss of consciousness, deteriorating consciousness, repeated vomiting, seizure, severe or increasing headache, weakness or tingling in the limbs, or unusual behaviour. Thereafter a period of relative rest is observed, return is graduated through stages with each stage completed symptom-free before the next is begun, and THERE IS NO RETURN TO COMPETITIVE OR CONTACT ACTIVITY FOR A MINIMUM OF TWENTY-ONE DAYS from the injury. The club will not abbreviate that period for a competition date, a selection or a participant's own wish, and a participant who conceals a head injury may be excluded from contact activity. SPARRING: sparring is permitted only with a coach present and supervising, only with appropriate protective equipment, only between participants matched appropriately for size and experience, and never as a condition of membership; a participant may decline sparring at any time without explanation and remain a full member. Pre-participation health screening is completed before any contact activity and the club may require clearance from a general practitioner. NOT A LICENSED PROMOTER: professional boxing in Great Britain is regulated and licensed by the British Boxing Board of Control. THE COMPANY IS NOT A LICENSED PROFESSIONAL BOXING PROMOTER OR MANAGER and does not stage professional contests; participation in any competition is through the relevant recognised governing body for the discipline and its own medical, licensing and age requirements. SAFEGUARDING: where any activity involves persons under eighteen, coaches and volunteers in regulated activity hold enhanced disclosure through the Disclosure and Barring Service, a designated safeguarding officer is appointed, parental consent is obtained, and the club follows the safeguarding standards of the relevant governing body; the safeguarding officer is [SAFEGUARDING OFFICER NAME AND CONTACT]. COACHING AND INSURANCE: coaching is delivered by persons holding [COACHING QUALIFICATIONS AND GOVERNING BODY], and public liability and personal accident insurance are held with [INSURER AND POLICY NUMBER]; a qualified first aider and a first aid kit are present at every session. WEIGHT MANAGEMENT: THE CLUB DOES NOT SUPERVISE, ADVISE ON OR FACILITATE RAPID WEIGHT LOSS OR DEHYDRATION PRACTICES for competition, which carry serious risk, and will not enter a participant whose weight management appears unsafe. SINGLE-SEX PROVISION: participation is restricted to women. The Equality Act 2010 permits the restriction of participation in a gender-affected activity where the physical strength, stamina or physique of average persons of one sex would put them at a disadvantage, and combat sport is such an activity; the club relies on that provision. NO OUTCOME IS GUARANTEED as to skill, selection, competitive result or fitness, and participation in combat sport carries an inherent risk of injury which cannot be eliminated.
